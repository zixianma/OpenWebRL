How this harness works

1 · ObserveDOM text, controls and recent recorded actions
2 · ChooseKev operation and target probabilities; argmax policy
3 · Fill if neededGPT-4.1-mini generates text for the selected field
4 · ExecuteHarness checks the cached DOM target and records accepted actions
5 · Judgeo4-mini / AgentTrek sees the final image and executed history

Kev receives DOM text, not screenshots. Screenshots are saved for review and judging. DONE is an actor stop signal, not a success verdict. The canonical judge is lenient; its verdict does not independently establish every task requirement.
