SFT proposes; Jev and Kev choose

Ten matched Online-Mind2Web tasks, four fresh browser cohorts. This is separate from the direct Jev/Kev experiment. The snapshot before the additional hour preserves the earlier interrupted traces.

What the models receive

The SFT actor sees the screenshot and full history. In selector conditions it samples five independent full reasoning/action proposals. Jev or Kev receives those proposals plus page text, element geometry and the last five selected actions. It returns a probability distribution over candidates 1–5. The chosen actor action, including typing text, passes through unchanged.

The SFT-only control generates and executes one proposal with the matching candidate-0 seed. The terminal o4-mini/AgentTrek judge sees a fresh final screenshot and the action history. Its success criteria are lenient; scores are judge verdicts.
