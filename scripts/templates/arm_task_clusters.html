How the proposed sampler works

First cover every website. Allocate further slots in proportion to the square root of its fine-cluster count, subject to capacity. Within each website, greedy weighted facility location chooses tasks that best represent the remaining semantic neighborhoods.

score(S) = Σ cluster c √size(c) × max selected task j max(0, cosine(center(c), embedding(j)))

Square-root weighting tempers large repeated families while retaining more support for a substantial group than an isolated outlier. The diagnostic uses each cluster center as a proxy for its members, with two central, three random and one boundary candidate per large-site cluster; smaller sites keep all candidates except audit tasks. Workflow tags do not impose extra sampling quotas.

Cluster round-robin cycles through groups, choosing central candidates first. Farthest-point maximizes distance from the current subset. Random is uniform within the same website shortlist. No final screening/training cohort has been committed.
